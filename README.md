# Bajaj Finance Ltd. – Strategic FP&A Dashboard

## Overview
This project analyzes Bajaj Finance Ltd.'s historical financial performance, develops FY27–FY29 forecasts, evaluates Bull/Base/Bear scenarios, and presents insights through an interactive Power BI dashboard.
The project combines Excel-based financial analysis, forecasting, scenario modeling, variance analysis, and AI-assisted research to support strategic decision-making.

## Project Objectives
* Analyze historical business and financial performance (FY23–FY26)
* Build a forecast model for FY27–FY29
* Evaluate Bull, Base, and Bear business scenarios
* Identify key performance drivers and risks
* Develop executive-level dashboards in Power BI
* Generate actionable business insights for management reporting

## AI Utilization
AI was used as a productivity and research assistant throughout the project to:
* Summarize annual reports and investor presentations
* Extract key financial and business insights
* Support management commentary and reporting narratives
* Assist dashboard storytelling and documentation
* Improve efficiency in financial analysis workflows
AI was not used to automatically generate forecasts; forecasting assumptions and scenario models were developed independently using financial analysis and business judgment.

## Key Insights
* AUM grew from ₹2.47 lakh Cr in FY23 to ₹5.10 lakh Cr in FY26
* Customer franchise expanded from 69.1 Mn to 119.3 Mn
* PAT increased from ₹11,508 Cr to ₹19,332 Cr over FY23–FY26
* Capital adequacy remained strong at 21.55%
* FY29 forecast projects AUM above ₹8.3 lakh Cr under the Base Case
* Bull scenario demonstrates strongest profitability outcomes
