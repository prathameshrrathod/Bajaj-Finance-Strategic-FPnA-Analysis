## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### Why was Bajaj Finance selected for this project?
Bajaj Finance is one of India's largest NBFCs with a diversified lending portfolio, strong growth history, and extensive public disclosures. This makes it suitable for financial analysis, forecasting, and scenario modeling.

### What was the objective of this project?
The objective was to simulate an FP&A workflow by analyzing historical performance, building forecasts, evaluating business scenarios, and presenting insights through executive-level dashboards.

### Why were FY27–FY29 chosen for forecasting?
A three-year forecast horizon provides a balance between strategic planning relevance and forecast reliability. It is commonly used in FP&A and budgeting exercises.

### How were the forecasts developed?
Forecasts were built using historical performance trends, management guidance, investor presentation insights, business assumptions, and analyst judgment. The model is assumption-driven rather than purely formula-driven.

### Why are Bull, Base, and Bear scenarios included?
Scenario analysis helps evaluate potential business outcomes under different operating environments and supports risk-aware decision-making.

### Was AI used in this project?
Yes. AI was used as a research and productivity assistant to summarize annual reports, analyze investor presentations, identify business drivers, assist documentation, and support dashboard storytelling.
AI was not used to automatically generate forecasts or financial conclusions.

### Why are some metrics based on assumptions?
Certain metrics such as Net NPA, PCR, Capital Adequacy, and Leverage Ratio require detailed schedules and regulatory calculations that are not fully available through public disclosures. Reasonable management-guided assumptions were therefore applied.

### Why is this not a full three-statement financial model?
This project focuses on FP&A, forecasting, scenario analysis, and management reporting rather than integrated valuation modeling. The objective was business planning and performance analysis rather than valuation.
